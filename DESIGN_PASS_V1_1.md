# SCHTMap design pass v1.1

## Objective
Move the site away from the recognizable AI/SaaS visual grammar and toward an industrial atlas / research register.

## Visual changes
- Removed the oversized marketing hero.
- Replaced floating rounded cards with a continuous grid and rule-based layout.
- Replaced teal/navy SaaS styling with warm paper, charcoal, oxide-red accent and restrained blue.
- Removed most pill controls and rounded corners.
- Increased information density.
- Shifted labels, metadata and controls toward a compact monospace register style.
- Converted company cards into flat register rows.
- Converted company detail drawer into a dossier/data-sheet presentation.
- Reframed the LLM as a dataset query utility rather than the product identity.

## Public intelligence added in this pass
- Sector distribution for the current filtered view.
- City concentration for the current filtered view.
- Public-signal counts for capital, government, hiring and verified addresses.
- Native Activity page using the existing 35 tracked public events.
- Event-type distribution.
- Company cross-navigation from the activity ledger.

## Deliberately still internal
- priority_missing_count
- detailed research queue
- internal QA failures
- enrichment task management

Those remain editorial/research controls rather than public product surfaces.
